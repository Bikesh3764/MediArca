**Short description (160 characters):**
MediArca is an Indian clinic booking and live queue app. Book a doctor, get a token and wait at home until your turn. ₹0 platform fee.

**Long description:**
MediArca (mediarca.in) helps patients in India skip crowded clinic waiting rooms. Patients find a doctor, book a token for a shift and see their live place in the queue on their phone, so they leave home only when their turn is close. There is no online payment: the doctor's fee is paid to the clinic receptionist by UPI or at the desk, and there is a ₹0 platform fee.

For clinics and doctors, MediArca puts walk-ins and online bookings in one queue. Doctors call the next patient from their phone, receptionists add walk-ins and confirm tokens, and patients check in by scanning the clinic's QR code. Doctors can show if they are in the cabin or have stepped out, so patients always know what is happening.

Free for patients. Available on the web and Android.
